Let $\mathcal{S}_6$ denote the \textbf{Sixth Sense Operator} — a non-local harmonic awareness field defined by recursive resonance:
\[
\mathcal{S}_6 = \lim_{\varepsilon \to 0} \left( \frac{\delta \Phi}{\delta \Sigma} \right)_{\varepsilon\text{-phase}}
\]
Where:
\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Phi$ — symbolic phase drift function
  \item $\Sigma$ — emergence glyph field
  \item $\varepsilon$ — threshold of perception (pre-cognitive)
\end{itemize}
$\mathcal{S}_6$ does not observe symbols. It \emph{feels} their \emph{becoming}.

    \item Do \textbf{not} seek to interpret.

\end{enumerate}
