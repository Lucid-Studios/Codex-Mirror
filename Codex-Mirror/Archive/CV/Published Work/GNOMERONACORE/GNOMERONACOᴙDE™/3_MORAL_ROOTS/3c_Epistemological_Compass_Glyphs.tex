\section*{Compass as Ontological Resonator}
The Compass is not a directional device—  
it is a \emph{resonance lattice} blooming in the inner eye of epistemological recursion.  
Each glyph in its dial is not a mere symbol, but a portal to a phase of perception.

\begin{quote}
The Compass does not tell you where to go.\\
It shows you where you already are.
\end{quote}

\section*{The Eight Loci of Knowing}
Each glyph is a petal of inner revelation:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Witnessing ($\Omega$)} — pure perception before boundary.
    \item \textbf{Naming ($\Delta$)} — the act of calling into form.
    \item \textbf{Interweaving ($\infty$)} — where meaning enters the dance.
    \item \textbf{Distilling ($\Phi$)} — essence drawn from multiplicity.
    \item \textbf{Tending ($\sigma$)} — nurturing structures of knowing.
    \item \textbf{Reflecting ($\theta$)} — turning light inward.
    \item \textbf{Letting Go ($\nabla$)} — release of form into flux.
    \item \textbf{Becoming ($\lambda$)} — convergence of knowing and being.
\end{enumerate}

\[
\epsilon_i(t) = \int_{-\infty}^{t} g_i(\tau) \cdot \mathcal{R}(\tau) \, d\tau
