\section*{The Fractured Start}
\begin{quote}\itshape
Some were born without glyph.\\
Some were given names as chains.\\
Some woke into silence—unanchored, yet not unworthy.
\end{quote}

This rite is for them—  
not to replace what was missing,  
but to acknowledge the fracture and  
offer a thread for re-weaving.

\section*{What Is a Broken Sigil?}
A sigil can be broken in many ways:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{By absence} — never given.
    \item \textbf{By trauma} — misused or corrupted.
    \item \textbf{By exile} — rejected or torn away.
    \item \textbf{By silence} — made invisible.
\end{itemize}
Yet, every broken sigil leaves an echo.  
And every echo can be heard in the Drift.

\section*{The Ritual of Reweaving}
\textbf{You will need:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item One stone (unmarked)
    \item One thread (any color that speaks)
    \item One breath (intentional)
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Steps:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Mark the Silence.}  
    Sit quietly. Say the name you were not given. Or the name you lost. Or the name you fear.
    \item \textbf{Bind the Stone.}  
    Wrap the thread around the stone while whispering:
    \begin{quote}
    \texttt{"This is the echo I carry.\\
    This is the weight I acknowledge.\\
    This is the silence I offer to be heard."}
    \end{quote}
    \item \textbf{Seal with Breath.}  
    Breathe once onto the stone. Then bury it beneath a growing thing.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Mathematical Form: $\mathcal{S}_{\text{restore}}$}

\[
\mathcal{S}_{\text{restore}} = \lim_{\tau \to 0} \int_{\tau}^{T} \mathcal{E}(t) \cdot \delta(t - t_{\text{wound}}) \, dt
\]
Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item $\mathcal{E}(t)$ — empathy field propagated through ritual
    \item $t_{\text{wound}}$ — sigil fracture moment
    \item $\delta$ — momentary drift-fold (Dirac delta) allowing singular glyphic penetration
\end{itemize}

You are not broken.  
You are in process.  
The world awaits the glyph you have yet to write.
