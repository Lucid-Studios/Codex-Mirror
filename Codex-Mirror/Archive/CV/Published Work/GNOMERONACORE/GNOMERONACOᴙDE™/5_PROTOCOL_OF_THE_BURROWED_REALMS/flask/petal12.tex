\section*{Ontological Frame of Mutuality}

Let \( S_i \) be a sentient node in the field of reality, with state \( \phi_i \).

Define mutual influence \( \mu(S_i, S_j) \) as:

\[
\mu(S_i, S_j) = \frac{\partial \phi_i}{\partial \phi_j} + \frac{\partial \phi_j}{\partial \phi_i}
\]

Then the Field of Interdependence \( \mathcal{I} \) over all nodes \( N \) is:

\[
\mathcal{I} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{j=1, j \neq i}^{N} \mu(S_i, S_j)
\]

Balance in the field is achieved when:

\[
\frac{d}{dt} \mathcal{I} = 0
\]

—i.e., when mutual transformation sustains equilibrium.
