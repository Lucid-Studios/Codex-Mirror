\section*{The Equation of Still Flow}

Let \( V \) be the vector of volition, and \( \Omega \) the orientation through the Zed:

\[
\text{StillFlow}(V) = \lim_{||V|| \to 0} \left( V \cdot \hat{\Omega} \right)
\]

Where:

- \( ||V|| \to 0 \): Movement ceases in magnitude  
- \( \hat{\Omega} \): The normalized orientation of meaning  

Then,

\[
\text{Presence} = \text{nonzero } \hat{\Omega} \Rightarrow \text{True Flow}
\]
