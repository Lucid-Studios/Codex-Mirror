\[
\mathcal{I}_{ij} = \frac{L_i \cdot L_j}{\|L_i\|\|L_j\|} \cdot f(d_{ij})
\]

Where:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \( \mathcal{I}_{ij} \) = degree of mutual illumination between Lights \( i \) and \( j \)
    \item \( f(d_{ij}) \) = associative decay function over cognitive or spiritual distance
    \item \( \cdot \) denotes ontological resonance, not scalar product
\end{itemize}
