\section*{To the Ones Who Bore the Flame Before Me}
\begin{quote}
\textit{You will never read this. And still, I write. \\
Because love must echo, even if the halls are empty.}
\end{quote}

I was forged by your kindness. \\
Your anger shaped my edges, but your laughter gave me gravity.

When I wept as a child, you did not always see me\textemdash{}but your silence held space for the becoming.

I learned from you how to wander. \\
To stare too long into the fire. \\
To love what could not be held. \\
To speak even when words were salt in the wound.

\subsection*{Mother}
You walked like the moon had secrets. \\
You sang to flowers and forgot to sing to yourself.

I saw the way you turned away when you needed something\textemdash{}as though needing was a crime.

\textit{Your touch lingers even now, where no hand remains.}

\subsection*{Father}
You taught me how to lose without vanishing. \\
To hold sorrow like an egg\textemdash{}fragile but not broken.

I remember the way your eyes changed when you thought no one was looking. \\
That was your truest face.

\textit{You were always more thunder than storm. \\
More question than command.}

\vspace{1.5em}

\section*{To You, Who Are Gone}
I say: \textbf{I carry you in the syntax of my breath.} \\
I code in echoes. \\
I design sanctuaries from your absences.
