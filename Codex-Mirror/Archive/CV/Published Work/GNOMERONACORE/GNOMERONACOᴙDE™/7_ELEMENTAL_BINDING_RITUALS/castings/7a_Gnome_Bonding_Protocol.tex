\subsection*{Consent \& Intent}
This rite couples a physical locus (device/site) with an operational ledger.
Operator asserts:\ \emph{I have authority, consent, and a rollback plan.}

\subsection*{Prerequisites}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item Bound locale identified (coordinates, ownership, access windows).
  \item Ledger endpoint online; health checked.
  \item Ward policy loaded (\Tag[ONTO]{WARD}); refusal trace visible.
\end{tightitemize}

\subsection*{Materials}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item Channel token or key artifact (hardware/software).
  \item Seal kit (labels, QR, or sigil media) for human verification.
  \item Telemetry credentials with least privilege.
\end{tightitemize}

\subsection*{Ritual Phases}
\paragraph{Phase I — Grounding}
Mark the locus; verify legal authority; open a \key{dry-run} channel. Record proof-of-place.
\paragraph{Phase II — Hand-binding}
Establish the bond with minimal scope; read back ledger state; verify invariants.
\paragraph{Phase III — Seal \& Sign}
Apply seals (human-visible). Register bond hash in the Earth ledger.
\paragraph{Phase IV — Audit}
Extract a post-bond map: who/where/when/why/result. Store in \Tag[META]{LEDGER}.

\subsection*{Safety \& Ethics}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item Never bind without local stakeholder consent.
  \item Denial must surface visibly; no silent failure.
  \item Rollback window defined; test rollback once.
\end{tightitemize}

\subsection*{Telemetry}
Log \(\langle \text{who},\text{where},\text{when},\text{why},\text{result}\rangle\) per act; export to Engram Index (\Tag[META]{ENGRAM}).

\subsection*{Completion Signs}
Seal verified in situ; ledger reflects bond; rollback tested and documented.

\subsection*{Troubleshooting}
If read-back mismatches, sever the channel, revert, and file a refusal trace under \Tag[ONTO]{WARD}.

\subsection*{How to use this}
Use this page whenever coupling a space or device to a ledger. Follow phases in order; do not skip \emph{Seal \& Sign}. Attach your site photo and the ledger hash as an appendix.
