\subsection*{Consent \& Intent}
This rite configures conversational lanes (human \(\leftrightarrow\) system) with explicit
role/turn boundaries. Operator affirms informed consent for data flow and storage.

\subsection*{Prerequisites}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item Defined roles (speaker, counsel, witness).
  \item Policy for data retention, redaction, export.
  \item Accessibility and translation requirements gathered.
\end{tightitemize}

\subsection*{Materials}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item Interface keys (auth tokens or ritual glyphs).
  \item Session manifest: purposes, boundaries, exit conditions.
\end{tightitemize}

\subsection*{Ritual Phases}
\paragraph{Phase I — Opening the Water}
Announce scope, rights, and exit. Display refusal path.
\paragraph{Phase II — Flow Agreement}
Set turn windows, escalation clauses, and summarization cadence.
\paragraph{Phase III — Immersion}
Activate the interface. Bind \key{Context} \((\mathbf{c})\) to flow registers; confirm echo accuracy.
\paragraph{Phase IV — Decanting}
Close the session; produce a signed synopsis; route to retention or deletion.

\subsection*{Safety \& Ethics}
\begin{tightitemize}
  \item Never force a session; honor quiet and exit.
  \item Avoid hidden inference; make summaries inspectable.
  \item Align export with participant expectations.
\end{tightitemize}

\subsection*{Telemetry}
Emit session start/stop, role changes, and refusal events; store negotiated policy snapshot with the record.

\subsection*{Completion Signs}
Participants acknowledge receipt of the synopsis; export pipeline succeeds or cleanly refuses.

\subsection*{Troubleshooting}
If echo drift occurs, pause immersion, surface the mismatch, and renegotiate registers before resuming.

\subsection*{How to use this}
Invoke 7b whenever you open conversational lanes. Paste this scaffold into your session doc; fill the Flow Agreement table and attach the signed synopsis.
