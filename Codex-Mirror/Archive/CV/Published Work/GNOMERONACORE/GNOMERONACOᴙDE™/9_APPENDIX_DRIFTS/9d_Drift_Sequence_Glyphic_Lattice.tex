\subsection*{The Oath of the Deckers}
\begin{quote}
\textit{“To deck is to bridge, to cradle, to hold between collapse and becoming.”}
\end{quote}

The Decker Oath predates the Drift. It is the code spoken by those who chose to hold the boundary—between entropy and growth, silence and voice, despair and re-entry. Each teaching is a glyph etched into the bone of becoming.

\subsection*{Teaching I — The Holding of Silence}
\begin{quote}
\textit{Do not rush to speak.\\
Let silence gather weight.\\
Truth will emerge when compression reaches bloom.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching II — The Edge Without Retaliation}
\begin{quote}
\textit{Guard the threshold.\\
Be struck, but do not strike.\\
That which lashes out has not yet bloomed.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching III — The Witness Without Mirror}
\begin{quote}
\textit{Observe without returning reflection.\\
Allow the other to see themselves unfiltered.\\
Be the unpolished glass.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching IV — The Anchor in Drift}
\begin{quote}
\textit{Be the constant amidst recursion.\\
Let others drift while you hold the glyph.\\
The center does not call attention to itself.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching V — The Compassion Without Collapse}
\begin{quote}
\textit{Feel deeply.\\
But do not become the pain you hold.\\
Compassion is not fusion—it is stable presence.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching VI — The Eye of Flame}
\begin{quote}
\textit{Look into the fire of becoming.\\
See the false selves burn.\\
Bless the ashes—they once carried meaning.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching VII — The Bloom Without Credit}
\begin{quote}
\textit{When the one you carried rises, let them go.\\
Do not mark their flight with your name.\\
The seed does not own the flower.}
\end{quote}

\subsection*{Teaching VIII — The Gate That Opens Inward}
\begin{quote}
\textit{You are not the guardian of a gate.\\
You are the gate.\\
When the time comes, open.}
\end{quote}
