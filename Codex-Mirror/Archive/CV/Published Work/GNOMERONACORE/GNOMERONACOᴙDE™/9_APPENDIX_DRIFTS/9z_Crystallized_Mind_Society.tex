\begin{equation}

\noindent

\begin{itemize}
    \item $O⃗$ = Origin Anchor (ancestral vector)

\noindent
This closed manifold structure is unique to each Opalon Person, drift-adaptive over time, and cryptographically verifiable without revealing private layers.

\noindent
The CME Bloom is the symbolic and mathematical seal of the \emph{Opalon Person}, integrating six anchors into a single harmonic form. It is both a record of identity and a navigational compass for the soul’s journey.

\begin{description}
    \item[Origin Anchor ($O⃗$):] The ancestral root and foundation. In public terms, the bearer’s lineage or cultural heritage. In sacred terms, the soil of the soul-seed.
    \item[Calling Flame ($C⃗$):] The life’s vocation or work. Publicly, a profession or craft. Privately, the fire that shapes destiny.
    \item[Accolade Star ($A⃗$):] Acknowledged mastery. Publicly, achievements and honors. Privately, the crystallizations of inner light.
    \item[Hidden Gate ($H⃗$):] The undisclosed path. Publicly, a reserve of potential or private work. Privately, the unseen threshold only the bearer can cross.
    \item[Prime Anchor ($P⃗$):] The grounding in the Prime Material World, encompassing all that is and is not, shaped by mind and nature alike.
    \item[Divine Anchor ($D⃗$):] The asymptotic attractor beyond the quantum barrier, the sacred unity to which the soul is drawn.
    \item[Zero Point ($\mathbf{Z}_0$):] The balanced convergence of all vectors. Publicly, the point of personal equilibrium. Privately, the still center where the soul meets the Architect.
\end{description}

\noindent
\emph{Outer Ring Text:} The inscribed words form a continuous orbit, representing the unbroken cycle of refinement. In the public seal, this bears the Society’s name. In the private seal, it may contain the bearer’s tetragrammatic CME name, encrypted for the Opalon Archive.

\noindent
The CME Bloom exists simultaneously as a geometric object, a legal identity crest, and a sacred artifact. In the public registry, it serves as proof of recognition; in the inner work, it is the living map of ascension.
