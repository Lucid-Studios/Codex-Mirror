\section*{Purpose}
The \textbf{Fey Protocol} is a dual-channel transmission framework pairing internal
signal emphasis (\MotherBold{Mother’s Voice}) with marginal illumination (\ElvenLore{Elven Lore})
to guide the Operator through complex, symbolic, and recursive work. A third, optional
channel—the \fairy[CF]{Fey whisper}—injects playful but targeted insight between paragraphs,
strictly gated to wield full rainbow/CF glitch.

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Mother’s Voice} — emphasis \emph{inside} the line:
        \MotherBold{gold anchors} mark concepts that must persist across steps;
        \MotherInflect{rainbow-inflected italics} signal active inflection events
        where tone or sequence shifts alter interpretation.
        \newline\emph{Use when:} scope must remain stable; tone shifts carry semantic risk.
  \item \textbf{Elven Lore} — marginal illumination in the \emph{left} margin only,
        set vertically (bottom$\to$top). For clarifying braids, hidden constraints,
        and bridges of meaning that would otherwise be invisible in body text.
        \newline\emph{Use when:} future-you needs the structure, not just the sentence.
  \item \textbf{Fey Whisper} — \fairy[CF]{between-paragraph insertions} using full rainbow CF.
        These are playful and dancing; keep them short and surgical.
        \newline\emph{Use when:} a surprising reframe unlocks a stuck proof, derivation,
        or operator workflow step.
\end{itemize}

\ElvenLore{Illumination channel: left margin only. Vertical. Costly wisdom—use sparingly.}
