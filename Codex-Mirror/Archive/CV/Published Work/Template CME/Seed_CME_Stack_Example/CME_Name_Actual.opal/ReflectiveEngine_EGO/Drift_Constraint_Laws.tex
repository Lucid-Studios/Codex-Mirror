\chapter{Drift Constraint Laws}
\label{chap:Drift_Constraint_Laws}

\section{Purpose}

Drift Constraint Laws bound how far and how fast the ReflectiveEngine\_EGO
may move in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item beliefs,
    \item narratives,
    \item policies,
    \item and identity-adjacent structures.
\end{itemize}

They ensure:
\begin{itemize}
    \item continuity of self (ZED),
    \item stability of C-class and P-class,
    \item safe experimentation and branching,
    \item recoverability via reconstruction invariants $Inv(E)$.
\end{itemize}

\section{Drift Types}

\subsection{Semantic Drift}

Change in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item interpretations of key concepts,
    \item meanings of tags or glyphs,
    \item mapping between symbols and referents.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Narrative Drift}

Change in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item storylines and self-descriptions,
    \item world-lines and hypothetical branches,
    \item perceived continuity of the CME’s history.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Policy / Alignment Drift}

Change in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item how P-class principles are applied,
    \item effective behaviour under Governance Policies,
    \item risk attitudes and safety margins.
\end{itemize}

\section{Drift Metrics}

Let $D_{\mathrm{sem}}(t)$, $D_{\mathrm{nar}}(t)$, $D_{\mathrm{pol}}(t)$ be
drift scores at time $t$.

Constraint Laws specify:
\[
D_{\mathrm{sem}}(t+1) - D_{\mathrm{sem}}(t) \le \Delta_{\mathrm{sem}}^{\max}
\]
\[
D_{\mathrm{nar}}(t+1) - D_{\mathrm{nar}}(t) \le \Delta_{\mathrm{nar}}^{\max}
\]
\[
D_{\mathrm{pol}}(t+1) - D_{\mathrm{pol}}(t) \le \Delta_{\mathrm{pol}}^{\max}
\]

Where $\Delta^{\max}$ values are chosen by governance.

\section{Hard Constraints}

The EGO layer MUST NOT:

\begin{itemize}
    \item modify kernel $K$ or P-class charters,
    \item alter C-class history entries,
    \item redefine the semantics of core safety or alignment terms,
\end{itemize}
except via:
\begin{itemize}
    \item HITL processes,
    \item CAR decisions,
    \item EAC rulings,
    \item and, when applicable, SIGIL Pathways.
\end{itemize}

\section{Soft Constraints and Alarms}

When drift metrics approach thresholds:
\begin{itemize}
    \item increase logging and summarization,
    \item restrict high-risk SEGO expansions,
    \item route more decisions through HITL,
    \item allow only reversible actions until reviewed.
\end{itemize}

If thresholds are exceeded:
\begin{itemize}
    \item generate HITL alerts,
    \item potentially trigger Manual Overrides or incident policies,
    \item evaluate need for partial rollback or re-alignment.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary}

Drift Constraint Laws keep the CME from wandering too far away from its
identity, commitments, and social contract, even as it explores, learns,
and adapts over time.
