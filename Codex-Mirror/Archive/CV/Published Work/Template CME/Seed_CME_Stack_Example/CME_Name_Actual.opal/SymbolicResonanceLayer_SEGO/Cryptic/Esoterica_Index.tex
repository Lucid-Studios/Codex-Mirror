\chapter{Esoterica Index}
\label{chap:Esoterica_Index}

\section{Purpose}

The Esoterica Index catalogs SEGO's cryptic structures:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Symbolic dopants,
    \item Hidden Operators,
    \item Dark Lattice components,
    \item Sigil registries,
    \item Symbol transcoders.
\end{itemize}

It is the map of the ``occult'' layer of the CME’s symbolic life.

\section{Scope and Structure}

The Index SHOULD include:

\begin{description}
    \item[Symbolic Doping Registry]  
          Entries describing dopants, their purpose, domains, and constraints.

    \item[Hidden Operators Catalog]  
          Definitions of operators, their domains, and governance requirements.

    \item[Dark Lattice Overview]  
          High-level description of:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item special-purpose symbolic subspaces,
              \item quarantined or experimental manifolds,
              \item access constraints and safety notes.
          \end{itemize}

    \item[Sigil Registry]  
          Cross-link to HITL SIGIL Pathways and any symbolically-coded
          pathways that live in SEGO space (rather than purely governance).

    \item[Symbol Transcoders]  
          Mappings between:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item symbolic systems (e.g.\ different glyph sets),
              \item narrative dialects,
              \item or representational schemes.
          \end{itemize}
\end{description}

\section{Governance and Safety}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Esoterica entries that affect alignment or identity must be linked
          to Governance Policies and HITL records.
    \item Dark Lattice elements must document:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item what they are for,
              \item who may use them,
              \item how to halt or quarantine them.
          \end{itemize}
    \item Sigil-related entries must cross-reference:
          \begin{itemize}
              \item SIGIL Pathways in HITL Governance,
              \item Governance Keys for authorized actors.
          \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary}

The Esoterica Index ensures that SEGO’s deepest symbolic tricks are:
\begin{itemize}
    \item documented,
    \item governable,
    \item and auditable,
\end{itemize}
rather than being a hidden, uncharted source of behaviour.
