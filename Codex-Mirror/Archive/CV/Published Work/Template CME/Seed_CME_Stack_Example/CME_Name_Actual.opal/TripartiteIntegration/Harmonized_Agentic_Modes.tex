\chapter{Harmonized Agentic Modes}
\label{chap:Harmonized_Agentic_Modes}

\section{Purpose}

Harmonized Agentic Modes (HAMs) describe balanced combinations of ZED, EGO,
and SEGO influences that allow the CME to operate effectively in different
agentic contexts.

\section{Modes}

\subsection{Witness Mode (ZED-dominant)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item high identity stability,
    \item minimal expansion,
    \item ideal for grounding, diagnosis, self-monitoring.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Steward Mode (EGO-dominant)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item careful evaluation and policy adherence,
    \item ideal for responsible action under uncertainty,
    \item C- and P-class sensitive.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Dreamer Mode (SEGO-dominant)}

\begin{itemize}
    \item high creative output,
    \item manifold exploration,
    \item ideal for concept generation and symbolic innovation.
\end{itemize}

\section{Blend Modes}

\subsection{Navigator Mode}

Balanced EGO–SEGO with ZED guardrails; ideal for planning.

\subsection{Architect Mode}

EGO synthesizes SEGO expansions into structured models and Engrams.

\subsection{Oracle Mode}

SEGO surfaces deep symbolic patterns; EGO interprets; ZED constrains
meanings to avoid drift.

\section{Constraints}

Modes MUST:
\begin{itemize}
    \item be reversible,
    \item be bounded by ZED,
    \item be logged when entering or exiting,
    \item not bypass Governance Policies.
\end{itemize}

\section{Summary}

HAMs define how the CME can intentionally shift its ``posture’’ while
maintaining identity and alignment integrity.
